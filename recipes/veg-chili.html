Instant Pot Protein-Packed Vegetarian Chilli

Prep Time:

15 mins

Cook Time:

25 mins

Additional Time:

5 mins

Total Time:

45 mins

Servings:

6

Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoons of Olive Oil
  • 1 cup of chopped onion
  • ½ cup chopped green bell pepper
  • ½ cup chopped red bell pepper
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1lb vegetarian ground beef substitute (such as Beyond Meat® Beyond Beef® or Quorn® Mince Beef)
  • 1 (15 ounce) can chilli beans
  • 1 (14.5 ounce) can diced tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons chilli powder

Directions

Step 1:

Heat oil in a multi-functional pressure cooker (such as Instant Pot®) set to Sauté. Add the onion and bell peppers and sauté until tender which is about three minutes. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add the ground beef substitute, cook and stir, until it's browned and crumbly. Add beans, tomatoes, and chilli powder. Hit Cancel.

Step 2:

Close and lock the lid. Select high pressure according to manufacturer's instructions; set the timer for five minutes. Allow ten to fifteen minutes for pressure to build.

Step 3:

Release pressure carefully using the quick-release method according to manufacturer's instructions, about five minutes. Unlock and remove the lid. Serve immediately and enjoy your healthy dinner.
